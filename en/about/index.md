### What I Do

I work in digital products and AI, both as a product manager and as a builder. If I had to summarize it in one line: I take business problems from zero to impactful products. **I understand it, and I build it.**

You can find the projects and products I've worked on in the [Works](/works/) section, and my CV is available [here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Dodg27zS_ABmYIZCwIlYvRtGIq5ZzSRa/view?usp=sharing).

### Background

My path wasn't linear. I studied Electrical Engineering, got a Master's in Control Systems, and along the way discovered machine learning, management consulting, data science, and eventually product management.

Each step taught me something different. Engineering gave me a systems mindset. Consulting taught me how to understand organizations and stakeholders. Data science gave me the tools to turn problems into solutions. And product management is where all of it comes together.
